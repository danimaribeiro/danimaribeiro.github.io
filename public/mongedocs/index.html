Step-by-step guides from your clicks

Monge Docs is a Chrome extension. Record yourself doing a task in any website, and it writes the how-to guide for you, with a screenshot for every step.

Add to Chrome

How it works

  1. 1. RecordOpen the site you want to document, click the Monge Docs icon, and do the task. Clicks, typing, selections, and page changes become steps.
  2. 2. GeneratePress Stop. AI turns the steps and screenshots into a written guide in English, Portuguese, or Spanish.
  3. 3. Edit and exportEdit the text, highlight or blur parts of screenshots, and export as Markdown with images (ZIP) or PDF.

Your recordings stay in your browser

Recordings, screenshots, and guides are stored on your computer, not on our servers. You can use your own Gemini or OpenAI API key, so your data goes straight to that provider. Read the privacy policy for exactly what is sent, and when.
